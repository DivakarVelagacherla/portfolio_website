THE JOURNEY

It started with a feeling, not a plan.

College. A cheap camera. No agenda. I just wanted to freeze things that made me feel something. Somewhere along the way, I realised I had a natural eye.

I shoot what catches my eye — the light hitting a hood just right, a tree stubbornly blooming in a parking lot, a quiet moment nobody else noticed. Photography is how I pay attention.

My favourite shots aren't the dramatic ones. They're the ones nobody saw coming — someone laughing too hard, a flower catching morning light, a perfect moment nobody planned.
